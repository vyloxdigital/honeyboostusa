Tongkat Ali Extract

Southeast Asian root with a long history of traditional use for male hormonal health. Studied for its potential to support testosterone balance, reduce stress-related hormonal suppression, and improve physical endurance in men.

Maca Root Extract
100 mg per gummy

Native to the Peruvian Andes, Maca root has been used for centuries to support energy, mood and male reproductive function. Research suggests it may improve sexual desire and physical performance without directly altering hormone levels.

Ashwagandha Extract
100 mg per gummy

A cornerstone herb in Ayurvedic medicine. Ashwagandha is widely studied for its adaptogenic properties — helping the body manage cortisol (the stress hormone), which when chronically elevated, can suppress testosterone and drain vitality.

🌸
Epimedium Extract
100 mg per gummy

Also known as Horny Goat Weed, Epimedium contains icariin — a compound studied in laboratory settings for its role in supporting blood flow and male performance. A traditional tonic used for centuries in Eastern wellness practice.

💪
L-Arginine HCl
50 mg per gummy

An amino acid that serves as a direct substrate for nitric oxide (NO) production in the body. Nitric oxide relaxes and widens blood vessels, supporting healthy circulation — a key factor in physical performance and stamina.

🫀
Beetroot Extract
50 mg per gummy

Naturally rich in dietary nitrates, beetroot extract works synergistically with L-Arginine to further support nitric oxide synthesis. Used by athletes to support cardiovascular efficiency, oxygen utilization and exercise endurance.

🍇
Grape Seed Extract
50 mg per gummy

Rich in proanthocyanidins — some of nature's most potent antioxidant compounds. Grape seed extract supports vascular health, helps combat oxidative stress in blood vessels, and completes HoneyBoost's comprehensive circulatory support blend.
