Tongkat Ali: The Traditional Testosterone-Support Root

Tongkat Ali — known scientifically as Eurycoma longifolia — is a tall, slender tree native to Malaysia, Indonesia and Thailand. Its root has been used in traditional Southeast Asian medicine for centuries as a male wellness tonic, particularly for vitality and reproductive health.

Modern research has focused on Tongkat Ali's potential to support healthy testosterone levels, particularly in men experiencing age-related hormonal decline. Studies published in journals like the Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition have explored its effects on hormonal balance, stress, and male reproductive function.

  • May support healthy testosterone balance in men over 40
  • Studied for effects on stress hormones (cortisol)
  • Traditional use for male energy and reproductive health
  • HoneyBoost includes the highest dose in the formula at 200 mg

Maca Root: The Andean Energy & Drive Adaptogen

Maca is a cruciferous vegetable grown high in the Peruvian Andes, where it has been cultivated and used as both food and medicine for over 2,000 years. Peruvian warriors reportedly consumed maca before battle for strength and energy — and modern men use it for much the same reason.

What makes Maca unique is that research suggests it may improve sexual desire and physical performance without directly altering testosterone or estrogen levels — making it a versatile addition to any male wellness stack. Learn more at Healthline's Maca Root Overview.

  • May enhance sexual desire and libido in men
  • Studied for energy and physical performance support
  • Does not appear to alter hormone levels directly
  • Traditionally used for centuries in Andean wellness

Ashwagandha Extract

Ingredient #3

Ashwagandha: The Stress-Cortisol-Testosterone Link

Ashwagandha is one of the most extensively studied herbs in modern nutritional science. An adaptogen used in Ayurvedic medicine for over 3,000 years, it is best known for helping the body manage stress — but its significance for male vitality runs deeper than that.

Chronic elevated cortisol (the body's primary stress hormone) is one of the leading suppressors of testosterone in men. By helping regulate cortisol response, Ashwagandha creates an environment where testosterone can function more effectively. Research overview available at Examine.com's Ashwagandha Evidence Page.

  • Adaptogen — helps body respond to physical and mental stress
  • May lower cortisol, indirectly supporting testosterone
  • Studied for improved sleep quality and recovery
  • Note: rare liver injury reported; consult your doctor

Horny Goat Weed · Traditional Chinese Medicine
Ingredient #4

Epimedium: The Traditional Performance Herb

Epimedium, commonly called Horny Goat Weed, has been used in Traditional Chinese Medicine for over a millennium. Its active compound, icariin, is a flavonoid that has been studied in laboratory settings for its potential effects on blood flow and male performance pathways.

Laboratory studies on icariin suggest it may inhibit certain enzymes involved in blood flow regulation — a similar mechanism to how prescription performance medications work, though at much lower potency. This makes Epimedium a traditional complement to the circulatory-support ingredients in HoneyBoost's formula.

  • Contains icariin — studied for blood flow pathways
  • Traditional Chinese Medicine use for male vitality
  • May complement L-Arginine and Beetroot's circulatory benefits
  • Caution advised with cardiovascular conditions

Ingredient #5

L-Arginine: The Nitric Oxide Building Block

L-Arginine is a semi-essential amino acid that plays a direct role in the body's production of nitric oxide (NO). Nitric oxide is a signaling molecule that causes blood vessels to relax and widen — a process called vasodilation — which is critical for cardiovascular health, athletic performance and male function.

In HoneyBoost's formula, L-Arginine works alongside Beetroot extract to create a dual-pathway nitric oxide support system. Read the science at the Mayo Clinic's L-Arginine Overview.

  • Direct precursor to nitric oxide (NO) in the body
  • Supports healthy blood vessel relaxation and circulation
  • Studied for exercise performance and recovery
  • Works synergistically with Beetroot extract

Beetroot Extract
Beta vulgaris · Dietary Nitrate Source
Ingredient #6

Beetroot Extract: Nature's Circulation Amplifier

Beetroot (Beta vulgaris) has become a well-regarded ingredient in athletic nutrition due to its high concentration of dietary nitrates. When consumed, these nitrates are converted by the body into nitric oxide through a pathway separate from — but complementary to — the L-Arginine pathway.

This dual-pathway approach is one of HoneyBoost's key formulation strengths: Beetroot and L-Arginine both contribute to nitric oxide production, potentially creating a more sustained effect on vascular health and circulation than either ingredient alone.

  • Rich in dietary nitrates — converted to nitric oxide in the body
  • Different NO production pathway than L-Arginine (synergistic)
  • Studied for athletic endurance and cardiovascular measures
  • Caution with blood pressure medication

Grape Seed Extract
Vitis vinifera · Antioxidant Shield
Ingredient #7

Grape Seed Extract: Antioxidant Protection for Vascular Health

Grape Seed Extract (GSE) is rich in proanthocyanidins — oligomeric compounds that exhibit some of the most potent antioxidant activity observed in natural ingredients. These compounds help protect blood vessels and cells from oxidative stress, which can impair circulation and hormonal function over time.

In HoneyBoost's formula, Grape Seed Extract plays a protective role — preserving the vascular gains made by L-Arginine and Beetroot by reducing oxidative damage to blood vessel walls. It completes a comprehensive circulatory support system.

  • High proanthocyanidin content for antioxidant defense
  • Supports vascular health and blood vessel integrity
  • Complements L-Arginine and Beetroot's NO production
  • May interact with blood-clotting medications
